About us

Three ways in.

Pure North Vault is a Toronto collector shop for sneakers and sealed trading cards. Small on purpose: everything is sourced, inspected and photographed by us, on the same backdrop you see on every listing.

Buy

Pick from what's in the vault today, or tell us the grail you're hunting and we'll go find it. Every listing shows the real item — not a stock photo — with size, condition and what's included.

Sell

Send photos and what you're after. You get a straight offer within a day, cash or e-transfer on pickup, and no fees taken off the top. One pair or a whole collection.

Trade

Sitting on a pair you've stopped wearing? Trade it toward sealed product, or the other way around. We value both sides in front of you and you decide if it's fair.
